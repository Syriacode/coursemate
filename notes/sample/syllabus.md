# CS 201 Data Structures: Syllabus

## Course information

CS 201 meets Monday and Wednesday, 10:00–11:15 am, in room 214. Office hours are Tuesdays from 2:00 to 4:00 pm in office 312. Come with questions about lectures, labs, or projects, and bring your code if you want help debugging it.

## Grading

Labs are worth 30%, the two projects 30%, the midterm 15%, the final exam 20%, and participation 5%.

## Late work

Labs and projects lose 10% per day late, up to three days. Work submitted after three days is not accepted. Each term you get two grace days, which waive the late penalty. Grace days must be announced before the deadline. Grace days cannot be used for exams.

## Regrade requests

If you believe a grade is wrong, submit a regrade request in writing within one week of the grade being returned.

## Academic integrity

You may discuss ideas with classmates, but write your own code. AI tools may explain concepts to you. Submitting AI-generated code as your own work is not allowed.
